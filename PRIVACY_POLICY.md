
# Voidex Store Official — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Voidex Store Official may process
information while operating on Discord.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on enabled features, the Bot may process:

- Discord User IDs
- Server IDs
- Channel IDs
- Role IDs
- Command inputs
- Moderation-related records
- Server configuration data
- Technical error logs

The Bot should not be configured to request passwords,
payment-card information, or unnecessary sensitive data.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Operate Bot features
- Provide moderation tools
- Save server settings
- Prevent abuse
- Troubleshoot errors
- Improve reliability
- Respond to support requests

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information for advertising.

Information may be processed by Discord, hosting providers,
database providers, or disclosed when required by law.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Data should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary
for its intended purpose.

For deletion requests, contact MARUF through the official
support channel.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Bot may interact with Discord and other third-party services.
Their own privacy policies may apply.

## 6. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features
or data practices change.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact MARUF through the official
support channel.
